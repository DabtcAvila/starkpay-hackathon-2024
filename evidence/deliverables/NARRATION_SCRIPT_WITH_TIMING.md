## Audio Quality Requirements

### Recording Standards
- **Format**: 48kHz/24-bit minimum (professional standard)
- **Environment**: Noise floor below -60dB
- **Consistency**: ±2dB volume variation maximum
- **Clarity**: No mouth sounds, breathing audible only between sentences

### Microphone Positioning
- **Distance**: 6-8 inches from mouth
- **Angle**: Slightly off-axis to reduce plosives
- **Height**: Microphone at mouth level
- **Stability**: No handling noise during recording

### Post-Recording Audio Checklist
- [ ] No background noise audible
- [ ] Consistent volume throughout
- [ ] Clear pronunciation of all technical terms
- [ ] Natural breathing between sentences
- [ ] No audio clipping or distortion
- [ ] Proper sync with video timing
