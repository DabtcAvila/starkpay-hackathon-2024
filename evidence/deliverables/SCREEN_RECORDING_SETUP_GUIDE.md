## Option 1: Native iOS Screen Recording Setup

### Initial Device Configuration

#### 1. Enable Screen Recording
```
Settings → Control Center → Customize Controls → Add "Screen Recording"
```

#### 2. Configure Screen Recording Settings
- Open **Control Center** (swipe down from top-right on iPhone X+, up from bottom on older devices)
- **Long press** the Screen Recording button (circle icon)
- Select recording options:
  - ✅ **Microphone Audio**: ON (for narration)
  - ❌ **System Audio**: OFF (unless specifically needed)

#### 3. Device Optimization
```
Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never (for recording session)
Settings → Do Not Disturb → Enable manually
Settings → Notifications → Turn off for all non-essential apps
Settings → Camera → Record Video → 4K at 60 fps (if available)
```

#### 4. Audio Setup
- Connect **wired headphones with microphone** for best audio quality
- Alternatively: Use **AirPods Pro** for wireless convenience
- Test microphone levels before recording
- Speak 12 inches from device for optimal pickup

### Recording Process

#### Step 1: Pre-Recording Checklist
- [ ] Device charged to 80%+ battery
- [ ] StarkPay app force-closed and ready to launch
- [ ] Screen brightness at maximum
- [ ] Volume at 50% (for system sounds)
- [ ] Do Not Disturb enabled
- [ ] Auto-Lock set to Never
- [ ] Clean screen surface

#### Step 2: Start Recording
1. Open **Control Center**
2. **Long press Screen Recording button**
3. Ensure **Microphone Audio** is enabled
4. Tap **Start Recording**
5. Wait for **3-second countdown**
6. Begin narration immediately after countdown

#### Step 3: Recording Best Practices
- **Speak clearly** and at moderate pace
- **Wait for animations** to complete before proceeding
- **Tap deliberately** - avoid rapid gestures
- **Maintain consistent narration** throughout
- **End with clear conclusion** statement

#### Step 4: Stop Recording
1. Tap **red status bar** at top of screen
2. Select **Stop** in confirmation dialog
3. Video automatically saves to **Photos** app
4. Allow processing time for high-resolution video

## Option 2: QuickTime Player Setup (Mac Required)

### Equipment Required
- MacBook or iMac with macOS 10.15+ 
- iPhone with Lightning or USB-C cable
- External microphone (optional but recommended)
- Good lighting setup

### Setup Process

#### 1. Mac Configuration
```
Applications → QuickTime Player → File → New Movie Recording
```

#### 2. Camera Source Selection
- Click **dropdown arrow** next to record button
- Under **Camera**: Select your **iPhone**
- Under **Microphone**: Select preferred audio source
- Under **Quality**: Select **Maximum**

#### 3. iPhone Preparation
- Connect iPhone to Mac via cable
- **Trust This Computer** prompt: Select **Trust**
- Launch StarkPay app to verify connection
- Verify audio levels in QuickTime

#### 4. Recording Settings
- **Resolution**: 1080p minimum (4K if supported)
- **Frame Rate**: 30fps minimum (60fps preferred)
- **Audio**: External microphone or Mac built-in
- **Quality**: Maximum available setting

### Advanced QuickTime Recording

#### Professional Audio Setup
1. Connect external USB microphone to Mac
2. In QuickTime: Select external mic as audio source
3. Use **Audio MIDI Setup** to configure levels
4. Test record 30 seconds to verify sync

#### Multi-Camera Setup (Optional)
1. Record iPhone screen via QuickTime
2. Simultaneously record presenter via Mac camera
3. Sync footage in post-production
4. Picture-in-picture editing for engagement

## Audio Configuration & Best Practices

### Microphone Options (Ranked by Quality)

#### 1. Professional USB Microphone + Mac
- **Equipment**: Blue Yeti, Audio-Technica ATR2100x
- **Setup**: USB to Mac, QuickTime recording
- **Quality**: Broadcast professional
- **Distance**: 6-12 inches from mouth

#### 2. Wired Headphones with iPhone
- **Equipment**: Apple EarPods, quality third-party headphones
- **Setup**: Direct connection to iPhone
- **Quality**: Good for most purposes
- **Distance**: Integrated positioning

#### 3. AirPods Pro/Max
- **Equipment**: Apple AirPods Pro or AirPods Max
- **Setup**: Bluetooth connection
- **Quality**: Very good with noise cancellation
- **Distance**: Standard wear positioning

#### 4. Built-in iPhone Microphone (Last Resort)
- **Equipment**: iPhone internal microphone
- **Setup**: No additional equipment needed
- **Quality**: Acceptable for basic recording
- **Distance**: 12-18 inches maximum

### Audio Recording Tips

#### Environment Preparation
- **Quiet room** with minimal echo
- **Soft furnishings** to reduce sound reflection
- **No air conditioning** or fan noise
- **Phone on silent** (other devices)
- **Inform household members** of recording session

#### Narration Best Practices
- **Practice script** multiple times before recording
- **Speak at 75% normal pace** for clarity
- **Pause between sections** for editing flexibility
- **Maintain consistent volume** throughout
- **End sentences clearly** for professional sound

#### Audio Monitoring
- Use **headphones** to monitor audio while recording
- Watch for **clipping** (audio too loud)
- Ensure **consistent levels** throughout
- **Test record** 30 seconds before full session

## Lighting & Visual Setup

### Optimal Lighting Configuration

#### Natural Light (Best Option)
- **Position**: Near large window with diffused light
- **Time**: Mid-morning to avoid harsh shadows
- **Weather**: Overcast days provide even lighting
- **Angle**: Light source perpendicular to screen

#### Artificial Lighting (Controlled Option)
- **Equipment**: LED panel or ring light
- **Position**: 45-degree angle above and to side of device
- **Intensity**: Bright enough to eliminate shadows
- **Color**: Daylight balanced (5600K)

#### Lighting to Avoid
- ❌ **Direct sunlight** (causes glare and shadows)
- ❌ **Overhead fluorescent** (creates harsh shadows)
- ❌ **Mixed color temperatures** (creates color casts)
- ❌ **Backlighting** (makes screen appear dark)

### Device Positioning

#### Stability Setup
- **Tripod mount**: iPhone-specific tripod adapter
- **Desktop stand**: Adjustable phone holder
- **Book stack**: Stable, adjustable height solution
- **Camera position**: Perpendicular to screen surface

#### Screen Angle
- **Slight tilt**: 5-10 degrees toward camera
- **No glare**: Adjust angle to eliminate reflections
- **Consistent position**: Don't move during recording
- **Comfortable viewing**: Natural hand position

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Audio Problems

#### "No Audio Recorded"
- **Solution**: Enable Microphone Audio in Control Center
- **Prevention**: Always test audio before full recording

#### "Audio Out of Sync" 
- **Solution**: Use wired connection instead of Bluetooth
- **Prevention**: Test sync with 30-second recording

#### "Background Noise"
- **Solution**: Record in quieter environment
- **Prevention**: Use noise-canceling microphone or app

### Video Problems

#### "Recording Appears Choppy"
- **Solution**: Close background apps, restart iPhone
- **Prevention**: Optimize device before recording

#### "Screen Too Dark/Glare"
- **Solution**: Adjust lighting setup and screen angle
- **Prevention**: Test lighting before full recording

#### "Recording Stopped Unexpectedly"
- **Solution**: Increase available storage, keep device plugged in
- **Prevention**: Check storage and battery before starting

### App-Specific Issues

#### "StarkPay App Crashed During Recording"
- **Solution**: Force quit and restart app, begin recording again
- **Prevention**: Test all features before recording session

#### "Biometric Authentication Failed"
- **Solution**: Use passcode fallback, restart recording
- **Prevention**: Verify biometric authentication before recording
