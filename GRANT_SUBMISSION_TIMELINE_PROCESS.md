## Risk Management & Contingency Planning

### Application Risks

#### Risk 1: Multiple Application Conflicts
**Scenario:** Multiple grants approved simultaneously with conflicting terms
**Probability:** Medium (30%)
**Mitigation Strategy:**
- Clear communication about other pending applications
- Negotiate compatible terms where possible
- Prioritize based on strategic value and ecosystem fit
- Maintain transparency with all program managers

#### Risk 2: Application Rejection
**Scenario:** Primary Starknet Foundation application rejected
**Probability:** Low (15%)
**Mitigation Strategy:**
- Strong backup applications to alternative programs
- Request detailed feedback for improvement
- Pivot to alternative funding sources (angel investors, VCs)
- Continue development with personal funding

#### Risk 3: Technical Requirements Changes
**Scenario:** Grant programs modify technical requirements during review
**Probability:** Low (10%)
**Mitigation Strategy:**
- Flexible technical architecture design
- Maintain development velocity to adapt quickly
- Close communication with program technical teams
- Alternative implementation strategies prepared

#### Risk 4: Timeline Delays
**Scenario:** Review processes take longer than expected
**Probability:** Medium (40%)
**Mitigation Strategy:**
- Conservative timeline estimates with buffer
- Continue development during review periods
- Alternative funding sources identified and engaged
- Milestone-based approach allows partial progress

### Contingency Plans

#### Plan A: Starknet Foundation Success (85% probability)
- **Timeline:** Begin development immediately upon approval
- **Focus:** Complete Starknet integration as planned
- **Resources:** $25K grant funding plus personal investment
- **Outcome:** Beta launch within 3 months

#### Plan B: Alternative Grant Success (70% probability)
- **Timeline:** Adapt development plan to grant requirements
- **Focus:** Modify technical approach based on program needs
- **Resources:** Alternative grant funding ($10K-$50K range)
- **Outcome:** Adjusted development timeline and features

#### Plan C: No Grant Funding (5% probability)
- **Timeline:** Slower development with personal funding
- **Focus:** Minimum viable product with core features
- **Resources:** Personal investment plus potential angel funding
- **Outcome:** Extended development timeline (6-12 months)

#### Plan D: Partial Funding Success (60% probability)
- **Timeline:** Phased development approach
- **Focus:** Prioritized features based on available funding
- **Resources:** Combination of grant and alternative funding
- **Outcome:** Incremental development with public milestones
